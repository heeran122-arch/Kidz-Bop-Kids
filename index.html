Group Rules

  1. Respect everyone.

    Treat all members with kindness and consideration.

  2. No personal business or personal questions.

    Questions about someone's food, parents, private plans, family life, or other personal matters are not appropriate for the group. Keep conversations focused on group activities and announcements.

  3. No spamming.

    Avoid excessive messages, memes, or off-topic content.

  4. Stay on topic.

    Keep discussions relevant to Kidz Bop Kids activities and announcements.

  5. Do not share private information.

    Never share phone numbers, addresses, or personal details of group members.

  6. Respect meetings.

    Attend on time and be fully present during group meetings.

  7. No fake announcements.

    Only verified members can make official announcements. Do not impersonate leadership.

  8. Do not start unnecessary arguments.

    Disagreements happen, but keep discussions respectful and constructive.

  9. Do not abuse your role.

    If you hold a leadership position, use your responsibilities wisely and fairly.

  10. Follow the rules.

    These guidelines make our community better for everyone. Please follow them.
